S-PLUS Antlia Atlas

Description

Atlas of the Antlia galaxy cluster produced as supplementary material for Lima-Dias et al. (2026, submitted). The mosaic covers the 73 S-PLUS fields analyzed in the study and was generated using data from the fifth S-PLUS data release (Lima et al. 2026, submitted). The RGB composite follows the band mapping described in Fig. 5 of Cardoso et al. (2026).
